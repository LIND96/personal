Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the DAEBAK fan app and other fandom music apps by the same developer ("we", "us", or "our") handle information when you use our Android mobile application (the "App"). All apps in this family share the same codebase and the same data practices described here (music streaming, offline downloads, photo gallery, quiz arcade, fake video call, and AdMob ads). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information You Voluntarily Provide

We do not require an account and we do not collect your name, email, or precise location automatically. You only share personal information if you choose to use these optional features:

  • Song request form: song title and link you type. Sent to our Google Apps Script endpoint and appended to a Google Spreadsheet ({ app, title, link, date }).
  • Global high-score submission (score > 900 only): display name, country (pre-filled, editable), optional phone number, score, date, and a random per-install device ID stored in on-device storage. Sent to our Google Apps Script scoreboard endpoint and appended to a Google Spreadsheet ({ app, name, country, phone, score, date, deviceId }).
  • Contacting us by email: your email address and message content.

b. Automatically Collected Data (On-Device Only)

The following counters and preferences are stored only on your device (AsyncStorage) and are never transmitted to our servers:

  • Ad/usage counters: play count, gallery view count, download count, extra download slots, ad-failure streak, free-ads-session progress and expiry, ad format rotation cursor.
  • Feature flags and remote config cache, active song, offline song list, quiz/leaderboard/event cache, call logs (max 30 entries), announcement dismissals.
  • Detected country cache (last-detected-country, 90-day TTL) used to pre-fill the high-score country field.

Downloaded songs and photos are stored in the app's private folder (DocumentDirectoryPath) or, only when you explicitly choose "Phone Gallery", saved to the shared gallery via the system media store. Private files are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

c. Data Collected by Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob (banner, rewarded, and rewarded-interstitial ads): may collect the Android advertising ID, IP address, device make/model, OS version, app package and version, coarse location (derived from IP), ad interaction events, and crash/diagnostic signals tied to ad serving. See Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data-usage information.
  • Country pre-fill lookup (ipwho.is): when you open the high-score form with an expired or missing country cache, the app sends one HTTPS request to https://ipwho.is/ so the provider sees your public IP address and returns an approximate country. Your IP is not logged or stored by the app; only the returned country is kept on-device for up to 90 days. You can edit or remove it before submitting.
  • Content delivery: song catalog, lyrics, news, gallery, video-call contact lists, and update checks are fetched over HTTPS from our static hosting and Google Apps Script endpoints. Standard server logs (IP address, timestamps) may apply at those hosts.

2. Camera Disclosure (Fake Video Call)

During a simulated video call, the app may show your front-camera preview on your screen only (picture-in-picture). No photo is taken, no video is recorded, and no camera data leaves your device.

The call itself is a pre-recorded entertainment/prank simulation ("Disclaimer: Entertainment & Prank Only"). There is no live person on the other end and no microphone audio is captured or transmitted.

3. Country Pre-Fill Disclosure (High-Score Form)

When you submit a high score, the app may approximate your country from your IP address via ipwho.is (HTTPS) to pre-fill the country field. Your IP address is not stored by the app; the detected country is kept on-device for up to 90 days. You can edit or remove it before submitting.

4. Use of Your Information

The information described above is used to:

  • Provide playback, offline downloads, gallery, quiz arcade, and fake-call features.
  • Enforce daily quotas and ad-gating rules (play/download/gallery-view limits, free-runs reset).
  • Show banner and rewarded ads (including the optional ad-free session) and measure their delivery.
  • Operate the song-request inbox and the global leaderboard.
  • Respond to support inquiries and prevent abuse.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share data only as follows:

  • Google AdMob / Google Play services: ad delivery, consent handling, and in-app-update checks, processed under Google's privacy policy.
  • ipwho.is: single HTTPS country lookup described in §3 (IP visible to that provider for that request).
  • Google Apps Script / Google Sheets: stores only the song-request and high-score rows you explicitly submit.
  • Legal compliance: if required by law or valid legal process.

The "Explore All Fandom Apps" section links out to Play Store listings of our other apps. Tapping a listing opens the Play Store; we receive no data from that tap beyond normal store analytics.

6. Data Retention

  • On-device counters, caches, downloads, and call logs persist until you clear app data or uninstall the app.
  • Detected-country cache expires automatically after 90 days.
  • Song-request and high-score spreadsheet rows are retained as app records; contact us (§11) to request removal.
  • Ad-related data retention is governed by Google's policies (see links in §1c).

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location (e.g., EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

  • Ads personalization: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Permissions: revoke Camera, Notifications, or Storage access anytime in Android Settings → Apps.
  • Optional submissions: simply do not use the song-request or high-score forms; using the rest of the app does not require them.
  • Deletion requests: contact us at wekarya.tech@gmail.com and identify the name/score or request entry to remove.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience fan app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal data (e.g., via the song-request or high-score form), contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information: HTTPS transport, no password-based accounts to breach, private on-device storage, atomic validated downloads, and scoped permissions. However, no mobile app or transmission method is completely secure.

10. International Data Transfers

Our App may be used globally. Content hosting, AdMob, ipwho.is, and Google Apps Script/Sheets may process data on servers outside your country. By using the App, you consent to such transfers subject to the safeguards of those providers.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when features or permissions change (e.g., new endpoints, new permissions). Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
